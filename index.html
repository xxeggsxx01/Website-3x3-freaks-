Who we are

The Olympic format
of streetball

Freaks 3x3 is a basketball community founded in Italy by Nicola Paradisi (founder & CEO) and led in Nijmegen by Mohamed Carlo Sow. We play official FIBA 3x3 tournaments in our own kit, run pick-up games in the city and take Dutch players to Italian courts. 3x3 has been an Olympic sport since Tokyo 2020.
