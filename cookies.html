Cookie Policy

Last updated: 13 October 2026

In short: this website sets no cookies and uses no analytics, advertising or social media tracking. It remembers one thing on your device, the language you choose, and never sends it to us. Because of this, it does not need a cookie consent banner.

1. Cookies

This website does not place any cookies on your device, and it does not load any third-party content (such as fonts, videos, maps or social media widgets) when you open a page. Our fonts are hosted on our own website.

2. Local storage

When you pick a language (EN, NL or IT), your browser saves that choice so the next page opens in the same language. This uses your browser's local storage, which works much like a cookie:

NamePurposeTypeHow long
f3langRemembers the language you chose (en, nl or it)Functional, set by this website onlyUntil you clear your browser data

This value stays on your device. It is never sent to us or to anyone else.

3. Do we need your consent?

Under the Dutch Telecommunications Act (article 11.7a) and the EU ePrivacy Directive, storing information on your device needs consent unless it is strictly necessary for a service you asked for. Remembering the language you picked falls under that exception, and we store nothing else. That is why there is no cookie banner. If we ever add analytics or other non-essential cookies, we will update this policy and ask for your consent first where the law requires it.

4. Links to other websites

Links to Instagram, WhatsApp and email open other services only when you click them. Those services may use their own cookies; their own cookie and privacy policies apply.

5. How to remove it

You can clear the saved language at any time by clearing your browser's site data for this website. The site keeps working; it will simply open in your browser's language again.

6. Contact

Questions? Email freaks3x3@gmail.com. See also our Privacy Policy.
