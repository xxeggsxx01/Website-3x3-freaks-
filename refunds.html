Refund Policy

Last updated: 13 October 2026

In short: you cannot buy anything on this website, so there is nothing to refund online. Payments and refunds for sponsorships are set out in each signed sponsorship agreement.

1. No online sales

This website does not sell products or services and does not take payments. Our kit is shown for information only and is not for sale through this website.

2. Sponsorships

  • Sponsorships are agreements between Freaks 3x3 and a business. Payment terms, cancellation and any refunds, including for a full year paid upfront, are set out in the written sponsorship agreement you sign with us.
  • The 14-day right of withdrawal under EU consumer law applies to consumers. It does not apply to sponsorship agreements made by a business.
  • If an agreed event is cancelled or we cannot deliver an agreed benefit, we will contact you to arrange what the agreement provides for.

3. If we start selling online

If we ever sell products to consumers through this website, we will update this policy first. Consumers in the EU then have the right to withdraw from an online purchase within 14 days without giving a reason.

4. Contact

Questions about a payment or your agreement? Email freaks3x3@gmail.com.
